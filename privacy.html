RailNOW Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how RailNOW ("the app", package com.yapps.railnow) handles your data.

1. Data we collect

The app does not collect, sell or share any personal or device data. It has no account, ads or analytics.

2. Location

With your permission, the app reads your approximate (coarse) location on your device. It uses it only to decide which of your two saved stations is closer, so it can show trains in the right direction. Your location is not stored and never leaves your device. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time; the app still works, and you can switch direction manually.

3. Timetable download

Once a day the app downloads a public timetable file from this website (GitHub Pages). The request contains no personal data. Like any web server, GitHub may record your IP address and device type; see GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores your two selected stations, your last travel direction and the downloaded timetable on your device. If Android backup is enabled, the system may include these settings in your device backup with Google. The developer has no access to this data.

5. Third parties

No data is shared with any third party. The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

6. Timetable data

Train times are public data from Israel's National Public Transport Authority. They are planned times and may differ from actual service. The app is not affiliated with Israel Railways.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

8. Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings to remove everything it stored.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
